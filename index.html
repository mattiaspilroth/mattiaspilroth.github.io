Principal OT Security Architect

Security failures in industrial environments carry physical consequences, not merely informational ones. Architecture that cannot survive production reality does not reduce risk. It creates the illusion of it.

I design security architectures for long-lifecycle industrial environments where availability, safety, and operational continuity define what security must become in practice.

My work focuses on identity, trust boundaries, and network segmentation patterns that remain operable under constrained connectivity, legacy infrastructure, and distributed operational ownership.

Architectural Focus

  • Identity Architecture: Access foundations designed for long industrial system lifecycles.
  • Operational Risk Engineering: Moving from compliance checklists to technically viable controls.
  • Network Boundaries: Segmentation patterns that sustain continuous operations.
  • Regulatory Translation: Turning frameworks like IEC 62443 and NIS2 into deployable architectures.
